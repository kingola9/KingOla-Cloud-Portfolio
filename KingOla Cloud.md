## 🔍 My Troubleshooting Approach

I use a structured approach to investigate technical problems and avoid relying on assumptions.

### 1. Understand
Define the problem, identify the expected behaviour, and establish the actual symptoms.

### 2. Gather Evidence
Examine error messages, configuration settings, command output, permissions, logs, and relevant resource states.

### 3. Isolate the Likely Cause
Compare the available evidence with the expected configuration and narrow the problem to the most likely cause.

### 4. Test a Solution
Apply an appropriate corrective action and assess its effect.

### 5. Verify
Confirm whether the original problem has been resolved and check for unintended consequences.

### 6. Document
Record the issue, evidence, cause or likely cause, resolution, verification steps, and lessons learned.

> **Understand → Investigate → Isolate → Resolve → Verify → Document**
